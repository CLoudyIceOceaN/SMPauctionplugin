SMPauctionplugin

Sell menu + auction house with quick buy, DonutSMP-style — for BladeSMP (Eaglercraft 1.8 / Spigot 1.8.8).

⬇️ Download SMPauctionplugin.jar

version 1.0 · needs SMPmoneyplugin (the installer grabs it for you)

⚡ Fastest way: one command

Open the terminal in your Codespace, cd into your server folder, and paste this. It installs the auction house and the money plugin if it's missing:

curl -sL https://cloudyiceocean.github.io/SMPauctionplugin/install.sh | bash

Then restart the server. Done!

What it looks like

Mockups — in game it's drawn with real Minecraft item textures. Hover the green pane in /sell to see your total; hover any auction item to see its price and seller.

Auction house — /ah

  • /ah sell 5k — sell the item in your hand · /ah diamond — search for an item
  • Click an item = confirm screen · Shift-click = bought instantly
  • Bottom bar: hopper = sort · ender chest = ⚡ Quick Buy · anvil = refresh · sign = search · chest = your items · arrow = next page
  • Quick Buy: save your favorite items on the gray panes (pick the item up, click a pane — you keep it). Clicking a saved item then buys the cheapest one on the AH instantly
  • Sellers get paid instantly, even offline

Sell menu — /sell

  • Drop your loot in, hover the green pane in the bottom-right to see your total, click it — paid!
  • Renamed or enchanted items are never sold by accident
  • /worth shows what the item in your hand pays

Change prices & settings

Edit plugins/SMPauctionplugin/config.yml on your server — every sell price, auction length, listing limit, menu title, and message is in there — then restart.
